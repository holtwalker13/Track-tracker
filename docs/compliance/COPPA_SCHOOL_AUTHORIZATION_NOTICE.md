# COPPA School Authorization Notice (Draft)

**DRAFT — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.**

## Purpose

For students **under 13**, Pro Gradus supports collection under the **school official / school consent** pathway where the school has authority to consent on behalf of parents for educational use.

## What schools must do

1. Designate an authorizing official (not a individual coach acting alone).
2. Record authorization in Pro Gradus (`SchoolAuthorization` with status `ACTIVE`).
3. Provide families any notices required by school policy and applicable law.
4. Limit collection to information needed for the athletic/PE program.

## What the product enforces

- Under-13 account completion and roster creation are **blocked** until an `ACTIVE` school authorization exists.
- Scanning a class QR code or selecting a coach **does not** activate authorization.

## Prohibited uses

Student data will not be used for advertising, profiling for commercial purposes, or unrelated AI training.

## Revocation

Schools may revoke authorization in writing; Processor will disable under-13 collection pathways for that school.
