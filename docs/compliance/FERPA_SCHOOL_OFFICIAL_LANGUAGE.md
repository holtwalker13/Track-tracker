# FERPA School Official / Data Use Language (Draft)

**DRAFT — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.**

Pro Gradus may be designated a **school official** with a **legitimate educational interest** when:

- The School executes the DPA and directs use of the service for PE/athletics programs;
- Access is limited to staff and students with legitimate educational need;
- The Processor uses data only for purposes specified in the agreement;
- The School retains control over education records and redisclosure.

Redisclosure by Pro Gradus occurs only to subprocessors necessary to operate the service or as instructed by the School or required by law.

Schools may terminate processing and request return/deletion of records subject to documented exceptions (litigation hold, audit, backup retention).
