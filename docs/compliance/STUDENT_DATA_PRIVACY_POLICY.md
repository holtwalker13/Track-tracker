# Student Data Privacy Policy (Draft)

**DRAFT — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW. Do not publish without counsel approval.**

## 1. Who we are

Pro Gradus (“we”, “us”) provides athletic performance tracking software to schools. We process student information only as a **school-directed educational service**.

## 2. Information we collect

See `DATA_INVENTORY.md`. Categories include identity, class membership, and performance records.

## 3. How schools control data

Schools remain the primary decision-makers for education records. We limit use to authorized educational purposes defined in the school agreement.

## 4. How we use information

- Operate testing, workouts, leaderboards, and progress tools
- Authenticate users and secure access
- Provide exports and privacy request support to authorized school officials

We do **not** use student data for behavioral advertising or sale.

## 5. Sharing

We share data with subprocessors listed in `SUBPROCESSORS.md` only to operate the service.

## 6. Retention & deletion

Described in `DATA_RETENTION_AND_DELETION.md`. Schools may request export or deletion subject to legal retention obligations.
