School Administrator Onboarding Checklist

What we need from you to launch your school

Welcome to Track Tracker — your school’s platform for measuring athletic and PE performance, running live testing days, programming workouts, and sharing progress with students.

Our team handles the heavy setup. Before you sign in, we build your school’s roster, classes (periods/sections), and coach accounts so every user already has a login. Your role is to complete first-time access, verify that the data matches your school, and finish the items below so coaches and students can start on day one.

Check each box as you complete it. Estimated admin time: 30–90 minutes, depending on roster size and corrections needed.

Phase 1 — First login & access

  • Sign in with your administrator credentials

    Use the email and temporary password from your welcome email. Open the school URL we provided (bookmark it for staff).

  • Set a personal password (if prompted)

    Complete any first-login or invite flow so your account is secure before sharing access with others.

  • Confirm school name and current school year

    From the coach dashboard, open School and verify the active academic year matches your calendar.

Phase 2 — Verify what we pre-built

We imported roster, classes, and coaches from the materials you sent. Please spot-check and flag gaps.

  • Review the student roster

    School → Roster: Confirm names, graduating class years, and gender filters look correct. Note any missing students, duplicates, or spelling fixes.

  • Review classes (periods / sections)

    School → Classes: Match period names and rosters to your master schedule (e.g., “Period 3 Weights”, “Track & Field”).

  • Review coach accounts and assignments

    School → Coaches: Confirm each PE/athletics staff member is listed and assigned to the correct classes.

  • Send us a correction list (if needed)

    Reply to your onboarding contact with roster/class/coach changes. Small edits can be done in-app; bulk fixes we can handle for you.

Phase 3 — Policies & launch readiness

  • Confirm student privacy preferences

    Decide how names appear on leaderboards (full name vs. hidden/anonymous for peers). Coaches always see real names.

  • Identify your primary Track Tracker contact

    One administrator or athletic director we can reach for roster updates, new hires, and end-of-year rollover.

  • Share historical marks (optional)

    If you have prior-season CSV or spreadsheet data, send it to the onboarding team or use in-app import tools your coaches can access under KPIs.

  • Approve the coach rollout plan

    Distribute the Coach Getting Started guide (PDF) and each coach’s login email. Coaches should not reset student passwords without your policy.

  • Plan student access

    Decide whether students log in with school-issued credentials or class join links/QR codes from coaches. Confirm acceptable-use expectations with your IT department if required.

Phase 4 — Go-live sign-off

  • Attend a 15-minute launch call (optional)

    Walk through School navigation with our team or your lead coach; ask questions before the first testing day.

  • Sign off that roster and classes are accurate

    Email your onboarding contact: “Roster and classes verified — coaches may begin KPI setup and testing.”

Questions? Reply to your Track Tracker onboarding email. Coaches should use the separate Coach Getting Started guide for day-to-day workflows.
